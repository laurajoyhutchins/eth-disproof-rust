## Important interpretation boundaries

- Compiled, elaborated, source-equivalent, proved, and independently audited are distinct states. **Never conflate them**.
- All twelve claim correspondences have been checked on these fixtures, but this is not a statistical statement about unseen LaTeX. Source correspondence is separate from actually proving a claim.
- Independent gold proofs do **not** count as proofs generated by Leanify.
- `RG02` and `RG04` are rejected on unsupported product-valued-function syntax; `RS01` does not define matrix `P` in the checker vocabulary; `RC04` is unread prose. These are coverage limits, not crashes.
- `PaperFormalizer` and `AutoformBot` have installation evidence only, **not mathematical trial results**. There is no fair three-way performance ranking yet.
- The parser accepted **8/12** original research fixtures, but this small and intentionally selected set is not representative of arbitrary research papers.
- The benchmark is on an isolated branch; it is not a production ETH proof, and it does not establish mathematical complexity improvements.

Leanify's independent kernel audit has now completed for **all eight accepted articles**, yielding one `Research<ID>.Audit.built` theorem per article. Source files are byte-compared against the original fixtures before and after each audit. The workflow also checks every generated claim and records the six function-denotation proofs. The next unresolved gate is a controlled three-way formalization run, not another parser-only smoke test. Keep the agents' authenticated formalization experiments in a separate, controlled cost cohort.

## False-theorem mutation checks

Three adversarial fixtures make exactly one mathematical clause change to an
accepted original paper. The mutation is checked byte-for-byte against the
immutable source using `benchmark/latex-to-lean/research/mutations/validate.mjs`.

| Mutant | Single changed assertion | Generated Lean result |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `MRG03` | `ac+bd = bd-ac` instead of commutativity | Original false statement preserved and refuted |
| `MRS02` | `e-o=x` instead of `e-o=y` | Altered claim refuted; unchanged companion claim verified |
| `MRC02` | `binom(4,2)=5` instead of `=6` | Original false statement preserved and refuted |

**[Verified negative-case CI run](https://github.com/laurajoyhutchins/eth-disproof-rust/actions/runs/43643205816)**:
all three mutated articles compile and elaborate, all three pass Leanify's
kernel source-to-document audit, and Lean proves all three altered claims
**false**. Eight independent proof/axiom receipts were checked; none contains
`sorryAx` or an unexpected axiom. Raw compiler, generated-module, proof and
audit receipts are available in the run's `latex-lean-mutation-evidence`
artifact.

The mutation suite is **separate from the positive 8/12 parser coverage score**.
Rejecting unsupported wording is distinct from preserving a false claim
faithfully. In this suite the stronger property was established for three
supported false mathematical statements. PaperFormalizer and AutoformBot remain
unmeasured on these negative cases.
