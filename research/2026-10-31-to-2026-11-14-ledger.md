# Two weeks of ETH work

*October 31–November 14, 2026. Reconstructed from experiments and GitHub history, not a day-by-day commit log.*

## November 1–7: find the actual recursion

We reconstructed the appendix's packet construction, Cauchy deformation, 2-adic coefficients, tapered Hermite interpolation, weighted modes and child contractions.

The first problem was immediate: the paper's sufficient recursion schedule does not activate at ordinary input sizes. Small examples reached the direct branch before demonstrating anything about the recursive savings. That is a **parameter failure**, not a successful execution of the promised algorithm.

[Recursion identity and scale](two-adic-recursion.md)

## November 7–10: stop hiding exponentials

The proposed polynomial-exact Sudoku and ETH factor elimination needed a complete resource audit. For

\[
B_k=\bigwedge_{i,j=1}^{k}(x_i\lor y_j),
\]

the count is simply \(2^{k+1}-1\). Yet an unlucky dense factor elimination can retain \(2^k\) table entries. Exact answers and faster native kernels did not prove non-exponential worst-case behavior.

Two more corrections:

- A large grid minor is not a lower bound for our method. Row-transfer DP already runs in \(2^{O(\sqrt n)}\operatorname{poly}(n)\) on the appropriate grid family.
- A whole group may factor from other groups after mode routing. Its *internal* factors do not generally factor. The counterexample is a \(2\times2\) matrix with determinant \(1/8\).

[Benchmark families](cost-model-and-benchmarks.md) · [The group counterexample](spectral-group-actions.md#a-two-by-two-obstruction)

## November 9–10: measurements have a cost

The selector/Gram experiments found a useful projected rowspace equality. Primitive row reduction, however, can enlarge integral row norms, even when the rank falls. Selecting independent rows from the **original integer selector system** avoids that particular height increase. [Draft #95](https://github.com/laurajoyhutchins/eth-disproof-rust/pull/95) records the argument on the historical Python research branch.

[Draft #94](https://github.com/laurajoyhutchins/eth-disproof-rust/pull/94) streamed exact Taylor measurement tuples instead of allocating the entire Cartesian pool. Less storage, yes. Fewer mathematical tuples, no.

This established a better question for every proposed spectral improvement: **what happens to the integer measurements and final reconstruction?**

## November 11–12: the machine bridge

Source-linked physical instruction exports and trace tools improved. A complete original counting graph was still missing. [Query draft #177](https://github.com/laurajoyhutchins/eth-disproof-rust/pull/177) targeted the real `QueryStreamProgram`; [composition draft #179](https://github.com/laurajoyhutchins/eth-disproof-rust/pull/179) targeted high-query, leaf, DFS and outer execution.

[Merged PR #178](https://github.com/laurajoyhutchins/eth-disproof-rust/pull/178) proved a conditional bridge from *identical physical Lean source graphs* to the same abstract primitive TM2 machine. It did **not** establish that Rust already emits the entire original graph.

This distinction matters: a graph identity theorem cannot supply a graph we have not constructed.

## November 12–14: one compiler, not several

[Merged #184](https://github.com/laurajoyhutchins/eth-disproof-rust/pull/184) introduced a fail-closed generic compiler-IR probe. A separate residual-expression experiment examined noncomputable definitions without pretending that an `Expr` DAG was executable.

The in-process Lean 4.34.1 → Rust work converged on one GMP/`Rc` runtime. [Merged #230](https://github.com/laurajoyhutchins/eth-disproof-rust/pull/230) supplied source-exact List cases; [merged #234](https://github.com/laurajoyhutchins/eth-disproof-rust/pull/234) reconciled Array and external-source paths.

At this dated snapshot, [#237](https://github.com/laurajoyhutchins/eth-disproof-rust/pull/237), [#238](https://github.com/laurajoyhutchins/eth-disproof-rust/pull/238) and [#239](https://github.com/laurajoyhutchins/eth-disproof-rust/pull/239) addressed mixed scalar/object projection and source-checked ownership. Their current merge state must be read from GitHub, not inferred from this note.

Three unchanged substantial external-program acceptances and the original ETH noncomputable closure were still separate acceptance gates.

[Compiler semantics and proof obligations](lean-rust-acceptance.md)

## November 14: the largest costs are not hidden

The quantitative appendix study put numbers on the bad terms: tiny \(\epsilon\), large packet representations, exact field setup, tapered precision, mode tuples and a potentially enormous direct leaf.

A local speedup is worth keeping. But no local speedup is a reason to call the full algorithm faster until we can run the full algorithm.

[The detailed worst-case study](../rust/lean_eth_reference/docs/worst-case-and-polynomial-overhead.md)

## What remains on the board

1. Prove the source-facing many-group packet identity in Lean, with generated routing and exact cutoff hypotheses.
2. Run one original-source nonterminal count through query, high/leaf, DFS and outer; compare full Lean and Rust machine states.
3. Measure active recursion end to end, including intermediate coefficient bits and peak retained memory.
4. Revisit spectral and field reductions only when their cost and source-correctness conditions are explicit.

No hidden brute-force fallback. No second runtime. No paper parameters changed without proof.
